Questions travellers ask

Short answers to the basics.

What is an eSIM?

A digital SIM built into your phone. You add a data plan by scanning a QR code instead of putting in a plastic card.

Will my phone work with one?

Most phones from recent years support eSIM, but not all. Check your phone's settings or its maker's website, and confirm the phone is unlocked.

Can I still get calls and messages on my normal number?

Usually yes. Keep your home SIM in the phone, switch mobile data to the travel eSIM, and use apps like WhatsApp for calls and chat.

When should I install it?

Install it before your trip while you're on Wi-Fi, then switch it on after you land.
